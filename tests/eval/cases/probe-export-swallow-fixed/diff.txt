    fix(sbx): read the workspace-image probe through one helper that keeps its message
    
    gb_vm_workspace_image_probe captures the probe stderr and hands back its exit
    status, so both callers tell exit 3 ("no image packed yet") from a probe nobody
    could read. `glovebox export` warned about neither before, and reported an
    unreadable probe as "no session here" for a workspace that may hold one.
    
    The prewarm consume test now asserts its own call log never appears; the log it
    shared with the adopt drive above already held that drive's ls line.
