    fix(sbx): say when the kata workspace-image probe could not be read
    
    gb_vm_check_clone_workspace_arg read every non-zero exit of the
    ws-image probe as "no image packed yet" and carried a seed in silence.
    Only exit 3 is that sentinel; any other status means the guest could
    not be asked, and a durable workspace inside it may be the only copy of
    the session's work. The seam now warns with the probe's exit status and
    still carries a seed, so the launch is not stranded but the reader
    learns why a transfer ran. The seam test asserts the warning.
